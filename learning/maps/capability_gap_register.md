## When it opens

The register stays **CLOSED (collecting)** while the current queue is learned. It opens when any one of these is true:
1. `maps/fieldguide_map.md` shows **40 or more chapters Defended**, or
2. every rep **R01–R48** in `NEXT.md` is in Done or explicitly dropped, or
3. you say "open the capability register".

**Early pull (R2):** one item at a time may be pulled before the gate opens when a build or a capstone needs it now (for example, Capstone B needs an AI-gateway evaluation, or pm-agent needs a registry). Mark it `pulled`. Of all the items, CG-01 (defend and refresh your own method) is the cheapest early pull: one Defend rep on work you already have.

**When it opens (or an item is pulled):**
- The **Orchestrator** first refreshes the prior work the item depends on (P-1…P-5: check dates and market changes; re-derive estate-specific picks generically), writes the item's book material (field guide edition 2: the evaluation and composition chapters, Part XI, appendices J–K), rebuilds and republishes, then sets the item to `book`. A rep without its consume step is not promoted.
- The **weekly sync** promotes at most **2 items per week** with status `book` into the `NEXT.md` backlog, P1 first, each with its 📖 cell. Evaluation and composition reps sit in FRAMEWORKS (inside OWN) and SYSTEMS, hand-built components in OWN · Rebuild, platform hands-on in TECH, Capstone C in the PROVE rotation. Lanes, rules and layout are unchanged (L-11).
- While closed, the weekly sync only reports the gate count in its sync note ("Defended chapters: n/55 · R01–R48 done: n/48"), and the fortnightly research radar keeps dated market notes on the register's component categories, so the refresh of P-1/P-3 starts from history rather than from zero.

D.1How to use this index

Your reference build records every ruling as a numbered entry in DECISIONS.md: a verdict, the reasons, what was rejected and the test that makes it falsifiable. Some of those rulings are principles you should be able to state cold in an interview or a client meeting; the rest are the history behind them. This appendix gives the first set grouped by theme, then the whole index with the chapters that discuss each entry, found by searching the book. Headings are read from ARB main at fa1c0a8 and pm-agent at 6a31831.
